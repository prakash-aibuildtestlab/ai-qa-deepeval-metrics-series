The Nova X1 smartphone costs AUD 899.
The Nova X1 includes 256 GB of internal storage.
The Nova X1 has a 6.4-inch OLED display.
The Nova X1 provides up to 30 hours of normal battery use.
The Nova X1 supports 30-watt wired charging.
The Nova X1 does not support wireless charging.
The Nova X1 has an IP67 water-resistance rating.
The Nova X1 has a 50-megapixel main rear camera.
The Nova X1 supports 5G mobile networks.
The Nova X1 does not provide satellite emergency calling.
The Nova X1 includes a two-year manufacturer warranty.
The Nova X1 is available in black, silver and blue.
