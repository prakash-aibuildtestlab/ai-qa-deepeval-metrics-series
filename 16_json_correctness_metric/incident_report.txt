Incident ID: INC-2048

On 27 October 2026 at 9:15 AM AEDT, monitoring systems detected repeated login failures affecting the Customer Login Portal.

The incident was classified as high severity. Customers in Victoria and New South Wales were unable to sign in for approximately 18 minutes.

The security team confirmed that no customer records were exposed. The incident requires escalation to the application security team.

The issue has been contained, but the investigation remains open.
