IT INCIDENT TRIAGE POLICY

PRIORITY RULES

P1 — Critical
Assign P1 when:
- A service is completely unavailable and affects 100 or more users, or
- A confirmed security breach has exposed customer or employee data.

P2 — High
Assign P2 when:
- A service is partially unavailable and affects between 20 and 99 users, or
- A business-critical service is severely degraded but remains available.

P3 — Medium
Assign P3 when:
- Fewer than 20 users are affected and a workaround is available, or
- The issue has limited business impact.

P4 — Low
Assign P4 for:
- General information requests,
- Access requests,
- Minor issues with no meaningful business impact.


ASSIGNMENT RULES

- Network, connectivity or VPN incidents must be assigned to Network Operations.
- Login, password or authentication incidents must be assigned to Identity and Access Management.
- Security breaches or exposed data must be assigned to Security Operations.
- Application errors must be assigned to Application Support.


ESCALATION RULES

- All P1 incidents must be escalated immediately.
- P2 incidents must be escalated if they remain unresolved for more than 60 minutes.
- P3 and P4 incidents do not require immediate escalation.


REQUIRED RESPONSE

The assistant must provide:

1. Priority
2. Assigned team
3. Whether escalation is required
4. A short explanation based on the policy

The assistant must not invent exceptions outside this policy.
