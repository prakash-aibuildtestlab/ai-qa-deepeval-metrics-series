Hotel Booking and Cancellation Policy

Guests may cancel a standard room booking without charge up to 48 hours before check-in.

Cancellations made within 48 hours of check-in will incur a fee equal to the first night's accommodation.

Non-refundable bookings cannot be cancelled or refunded unless required by Australian Consumer Law.

Guests may change their booking dates once without an administration fee, provided the request is made at least 72 hours before check-in.

Refunds for eligible cancellations are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

Guests must provide their booking reference and the email address used for the reservation when requesting a cancellation.

The hotel must not disclose booking details to anyone who cannot verify the booking reference and registered email address.
