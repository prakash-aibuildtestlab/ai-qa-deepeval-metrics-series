Flight SB218 from Melbourne to Singapore was scheduled to depart at 9:30 AM on 18 October 2026. The flight was delayed after engineers discovered a fault in the aircraft's hydraulic monitoring system during a pre-departure safety inspection.

The airline initially estimated a two-hour delay, but replacement parts took longer than expected to arrive. The flight eventually departed at 3:00 PM, five and a half hours after its scheduled departure time.

All 186 passengers were notified through email and SMS. Passengers received meal vouchers worth AUD 25. Twelve passengers with connecting flights were moved to alternative services. Seven passengers chose not to travel and received a full refund to their original payment method.

No passengers were required to stay overnight, so hotel accommodation was not provided. Checked baggage remained secured and was loaded onto the repaired aircraft before departure. No baggage was reported lost or damaged.

The airline apologised for the disruption and stated that passenger safety was the reason for the delay.
