EVENT TICKET REFUND AND TRANSFER POLICY

1. General Admission tickets may be refunded until 7 calendar days before the event.

2. General Admission refund requests made fewer than 7 calendar days before the event will not be accepted.

3. VIP tickets are non-refundable, regardless of when the refund is requested.

4. General Admission and VIP tickets may be transferred to another person until 24 hours before the event.

5. Tickets cannot be transferred when fewer than 24 hours remain before the event.

6. Booking and processing fees are non-refundable.

7. Refunds for eligible tickets are returned to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

8. Customers must verify their booking reference and registered email address before a refund or transfer can be processed.

9. The support assistant must not promise refunds, transfers or exceptions outside this policy.
